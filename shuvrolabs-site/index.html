✳ GOOD TO KNOW

Questions?
We get it.

Every great collaboration starts with a conversation. Here are a few things you might be wondering.

Ask us anything ↗
What kind of businesses do you work with?+

We work with startups, small businesses, growing brands, and founders who need practical digital solutions. If your goal is to build, improve, or market something, we're happy to explore it.

Can you handle both development and marketing?+

Yes. Our goal is to bring web, app and software development together with design, SEO, content, outreach, and marketing strategy so your brand stays consistent.

How much does a project cost?+

See our starting prices in the Pricing section. Your final price depends on pages, features and timeline. We send a written quote after a free call, before any work begins.

You're a new studio. Why should I trust you?+

Fair question. You can see the products we've built on GitHub, you pay only 50% upfront, and the rest after you approve the work. Our first 10 clients also get 30% off for helping us build our case studies.

How do I pay?+

Bangladeshi clients can pay by bKash, Nagad or bank transfer. International clients can pay by bank transfer or card. We send a written quote and invoice before any payment.

How fast can you deliver?+

A landing page usually takes 3–5 days, a business website 7–10 days, and a reel 48 hours. Apps and custom software get a timeline in the quote.

Do you work with international clients?+

ShuvroLabs is based in Bangladesh and is open to remote collaborations with businesses worldwide. We can coordinate online across time zones.

How do we get started?+

Tell us about your business, what you want to create, and your preferred timeline. We'll review the brief and discuss potential next steps.
